Product Recall Traceability

An invented e-bike drive-unit plant: 40,000 units, sixty days of imperfect shop-floor records, and a supplier's notice about five days of one capacitor lot. The genealogy is inferred with probabilities, the recall set is the cheapest one that leaves out less than half an affected unit in expectation, and it is set against the plant's own deterministic answers and the simulation truth. …
The plant, its records, the supplier, the customers, the field returns and the defect are generated by a simulator in this repository; no company, product or person here is real. The truth is shown only to judge the strategies, which never see it. No outside model or service is called.
